# Character continuity

Maintain approved character identities, wardrobe, voice selections and environments across references, shots and projects. A continuity ledger records what should stay consistent and which take changed it.

**Current status:** planned differentiation; no owned continuity engine or measured identity consistency is verified in this checkout.

## Scope and first delivery

Begin with versioned reference sheets, descriptive attributes and explicit links from shots to their approved references. Record wardrobe and scene changes instead of assuming every difference is an error. Add model-specific conditioning only where supported; a reference library alone cannot guarantee visual consistency.

Traceability: [IMG-05 and MOV-02 registry](../../docs/product/FEATURE_REGISTRY.md), [M5 roadmap](../../docs/product/ROADMAP.md), [movie specification](../movie-studio/DESIGN.md). [Images](../image-studio/README.md) prepare references, [video](../video-studio/README.md) uses them, and the [movie studio](../movie-studio/README.md) tracks changes across shots.

For the [English friend](../english-speaking-friend/README.md), offer a clearly disclosed synthetic or licensed male/female avatar. User-uploaded likenesses require consent and usage rights; possession of a photo is not permission to impersonate that person. Keep avatar preferences separate from private learning records.

## Dependencies and acceptance

Reuse the shared asset registry, consent records, provenance and versioning. Evaluate existing reference-conditioning workflows under the [source/license rules](../../docs/security/LICENSE_SECURITY.md); preserve human review of likeness and voice permissions.

CPU checks should cover reference versions, revoked consent, cross-project access and reproducible shot links. Hardware acceptance requires a fixed multi-shot evaluation set, recorded settings, review of identity/wardrobe/environment drift and reported limitations. **GPU continuity evaluation: NOT RUN.** Perfect consistency across arbitrary motion, lighting and long stories is not a supported promise.
