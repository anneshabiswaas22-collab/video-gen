VIDEOGEN — CREATIVE STUDIO + ENGLISH-SPEAKING AI FRIEND
Combined project idea, 17 November 2026 (Asia/Kolkata)
STATUS: proposal and feature organization, not an implemented combined service.

THE IDEA

One workspace helps people create original images, video, speech and editable
movies, and practise English with a friendly synthetic conversation partner.
Creators use the studio; learners can open a simpler speaking-practice screen
without learning media production. Shared licensed characters, voices and
scenarios can connect the experiences, but private learning data stays separate.

A learner chooses a disclosed AI man, woman or stylized adult avatar. They can
speak imperfect English, Bengali or another explicitly supported language.
The partner responds to the meaning, offers “You could say it this way...” and
lets the learner try again. For “Yesterday I go market”, it can suggest “I went
to the market yesterday” and continue the conversation. Unclear meaning gets
a clarification question, not an invented confident correction. Acceptable
accents and dialects are respected. Corrections can wait until a turn or the
session ends; constant interruption must not destroy confidence.

SIX FEATURES

1. IMAGE STUDIO (features/image-studio/)
Generate images and reference sheets, then add editing, masking and upscaling.
First proof: one genuine, permitted, decodable PNG with recorded model version,
seed, settings, time, hardware and provenance. References can support other
features but do not guarantee consistent generated identity.

2. VIDEO STUDIO (features/video-studio/)
Create and revise short clips using controls the selected model actually
supports. Keep input references and takes; support explicit failures and
cancellation. First proof: a playable MP4 with verified frames, duration, codec,
model/workflow revision, latency, resource usage and cost.

3. AUDIO STUDIO (features/audio-studio/)
Provide cleared speech, transcription, captions and media mixing, then evaluate
music, sound and dubbing. Share speech adapters with the learning feature when
latency and privacy fit. First proof: intelligible licensed speech and a
playable export with aligned captions. Voice cloning is outside initial scope.

4. MOVIE STUDIO (features/movie-studio/)
Manage scripts, scenes, shots and multiple takes. Let users replace a shot
without losing history and approve the assembled timeline. First proof: a
reviewed 60-second sequence with editable shot history. Longer films are
assembled and revised, not promised from one magic prompt.

5. CHARACTER CONTINUITY (features/character-continuity/)
Keep versioned, consented reference sheets, wardrobe, environments and voice
choices. Measure identity drift across shots instead of claiming perfection.
Avatar licenses must permit each intended display/export use. A photograph
alone is not permission to impersonate its subject.

6. ENGLISH-SPEAKING FRIEND (features/english-speaking-friend/)
Start with Bengali-speaking adults, English practice, text and push-to-talk,
editable transcripts, optional gentle corrections, replay, phrase review and a
simple licensed animated avatar. User camera access is optional and off by
default. First proof: a teacher-reviewed meaning-preserving conversation loop,
then measured recognition, speech output and an opt-in pilot. More languages
require qualified reviewers and evaluation data, not a universal promise.

SHARED FOUNDATIONS

Use shared accounts, consent, assets, capability descriptions, cost controls
and monitoring through explicit interfaces. Creative rendering needs async jobs;
conversation needs a separate responsive session path. A slow clip render must
not freeze a learning turn. Share eligible assets only with approval: using one
account does not authorize publication or training on private conversations.
Default to no retained raw camera/audio, minimal telemetry, clear deletion and
usable text/audio fallbacks. Do not hide credentials in clients or logs.

WHAT WE CAN BUILD FIRST

Complete the owned M0 CPU mock workflow and evaluate exact dependency licenses.
Mocks must be clearly labeled; a text receipt cannot stand in for generated
media or language-learning effectiveness. Then choose a bounded creative proof
and a bounded tutor proof, with their different tests:
- One permitted image workflow on owner-approved hardware; inspect the real file.
- Typed English/Bengali learning with teacher-reviewed examples, uncertainty and
  correction preferences; add speech after that interaction works.
Do not start six full applications at once. Reuse maintained infrastructure;
build the distinctive learner experience, editable projects and continuity.
No paid APIs, model downloads, training or GPU rental are authorized by this
planning document.

ROADMAP AND RESPONSIBILITY

Year 1 ambition, conditional on budget and staff: a small adult Bengali-English
pilot and the first genuinely validated image/short-video workflow. The owner
sets scope and spending; engineers implement; an educator evaluates corrections;
a privacy/security reviewer checks release boundaries. Release gates require
measured useful behavior, not a calendar anniversary.

Year 2 ambition, after evidence: better interruption handling, scenarios and
review; one carefully evaluated additional language; stronger shot continuity
and short multi-shot editing. An avatar/media engineer and speech specialist
investigate richer rendering only if learner value justifies latency and cost.
Optional lesson exports need explicit preview and learner approval.

Years 3–5 research: private on-device speech, low-bandwidth practice, additional
dialects and accessibility needs, richer cleared avatars, long-story continuity
and scalable workers. Researchers, teachers and specialist partners evaluate
experiments with an accountable owner, test, budget and stop condition. AI
coding assistance cannot replace human language review, rights checks, user
research or real hardware evidence. Breakthroughs are not guaranteed.

WHAT CANNOT BE PROMISED

Perfect translation of all languages; zero incorrect corrections; knowing every
intention; human feelings; guaranteed fluency or exam scores; perfect identity
in every scene; free unlimited GPU rendering; frontier-model parity; or a
coherent hours-long film from one prompt. Some are research problems; “never
makes a mistake” is an unsuitable promise however long we wait. A cinematic
video model does not automatically provide cheap real-time conversation.
Use text/audio and simple animation while ambitious rendering remains uncertain.

MEASURE SUCCESS

For creative tools: inspect real files; record revisions, settings, dimensions,
frames, latency, peak VRAM, failure rates and cost. Review continuity on a fixed
multi-shot set. For learning: measure meaning preservation, false corrections,
recognition problems, turn latency and transfer to unfamiliar speaking tasks.
Publish sample sizes and limits. Proposed metrics are targets until measured.
Test permission boundaries, deletion, consent revocation, recovery and spending.
A useful creation or a clearer conversation is progress; more model names,
folders and realistic faces alone are not.
